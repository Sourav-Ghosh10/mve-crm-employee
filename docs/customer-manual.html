Pulse Ops Customer Manual

Your complete guide to managing attendance, leave, schedules, reimbursements, and more with the Pulse Ops Human Resource Management platform.

🏠 Overview

Pulse Ops is a modern, full-featured HR management platform designed to streamline daily workforce operations for employees, managers, and administrators.

⏱

Real-Time Attendance

Clock in/out, manage breaks, and review daily & monthly attendance with live tracking.

🌴

Leave Management

Apply for leave, track balances across multiple leave types, and view approval statuses.

📅

Schedule & Shifts

View your weekly roster, shift timings, holidays, and off-days at a glance.

📣

Announcements

Stay informed with company-wide announcements, scrolling marquee alerts, and acknowledgment tracking.

💰

Reimbursements

Submit, edit, and track expense reimbursement claims with receipt attachments.

👥

User Administration

Create, edit, and manage employee profiles, roles, departments, and designations.

💡
Supported browsers: Pulse Ops works best on the latest versions of Google Chrome, Mozilla Firefox, Microsoft Edge, and Safari. JavaScript must be enabled.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about using Pulse Ops.

How do I clock in for the day?

Go to the Dashboard or Attendance page and click the Clock In button in the Attendance Hero panel at the top. Your session starts immediately and your working hours begin tracking.

What happens if I forget to clock out?

Pulse Ops detects incomplete shifts automatically. The next time you log in, an Incomplete Shift modal will appear asking you to enter the correct clock-out time and a reason. This ensures your attendance records remain accurate.

Can I apply for half-day leave?

Yes. When applying for leave, toggle the Half Day switch and then select either First Half or Second Half. This counts as 0.5 days against your leave balance.

How do I cancel a pending leave request?

Go to the Leave page, find your request in the My Requests table, and click the Cancel button. Only requests with a Pending status can be cancelled. Approved or rejected requests cannot be cancelled.

Why can’t I see the Users page?

The Users page is restricted to Admin and HR roles. If you need access, contact your system administrator to review your role assignment.

What file types can I upload for reimbursements?

You can upload PDF, JPG, JPEG, PNG, DOC, and DOCX files. Individual files must be under 1 MB and the total of all attachments must not exceed 5 MB.

How do I enable dark mode?

Go to Settings → Appearance and toggle the Dark Mode switch. The theme change applies immediately across the entire application and is saved to your browser.

Can I change the interface language?

Yes. Go to Settings → Appearance → Language and select from: English (US), English (UK), Spanish, French, German, or Hindi. The change is applied immediately.

How do I export my personal data?

Navigate to Settings → Privacy & Data and click the Export My Data button. A JSON file containing all your personal data will be downloaded to your device.

What are acknowledgment-required announcements?

Some announcements are flagged by HR or Admins as requiring acknowledgment. This means you must click the Acknowledge button to confirm you have read and understood the announcement. This is commonly used for policy changes, compliance notices, or important organizational updates.

How do I view my weekly schedule for a different week?

On the Schedule page, use the ← Previous and Next → arrow buttons at the top of the weekly schedule table. The date range between the buttons updates to show the currently displayed week.

Can I edit a reimbursement request after submitting?

You can only edit requests with a Pending status. Click the Edit icon on the request row, make your changes, and submit again. Once a request is approved or rejected, it cannot be modified.

🔧 Troubleshooting

Common issues and how to resolve them.

❌

Cannot Log In

  • Double-check your email address and password for typos.
  • Ensure Caps Lock is not enabled.
  • Try the Forgot Password flow to reset your credentials.
  • If using Google Sign-In, ensure you are using the correct Google account associated with your Pulse Ops profile.
  • Clear your browser cache and cookies, then try again.
  • Contact your system administrator if the issue persists.
⚠️

Page Not Loading or Showing Errors

  • Check your internet connection.
  • Refresh the page using Ctrl + R (or Cmd + R on Mac).
  • Hard refresh with Ctrl + Shift + R to clear cached assets.
  • Ensure you are using a supported, up-to-date browser.
  • If you see a “Session expired” message, log out and log back in.
🔔

Not Receiving Push Notifications

  • Ensure notifications are enabled in Settings → Notifications.
  • Check that your browser has notification permissions enabled for the Pulse Ops domain.
  • On Windows, ensure “Focus Assist” or “Do Not Disturb” is not blocking notifications.
  • On macOS, check System Preferences → Notifications for your browser.
  • Try logging out and back in to re-register the push notification token.
📅

Schedule Not Showing Correct Data

  • Navigate away from the Schedule page and return to trigger a fresh data fetch.
  • Ensure your timezone settings are correct in your browser.
  • If your roster was recently updated by HR/Admin, it may take a moment to reflect. Refresh the page.
  • Contact HR if your schedule appears permanently incorrect.
📎

File Upload Failing

  • Verify the file is under the size limit (1 MB per file for reimbursements, 5 MB per file for leave attachments).
  • Ensure the file type is supported (PDF, JPG, JPEG, PNG, DOC, DOCX).
  • Try compressing the file or converting it to a supported format.
  • Check your internet connection — large uploads may fail on unstable connections.
